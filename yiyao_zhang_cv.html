Research Statement
My research develops trustworthy autonomous decision systems that unite causal reasoning, reinforcement learning, neuro-symbolic inference, and multi-agent coordination. I focus on AI architectures that reason strategically under uncertainty, justify their decisions, and remain reliable in high-stakes environments such as cyber defence, financial systems, and scientific decision support. My long-term agenda is to build verifiable, aligned, and safety-aware intelligent agents that integrate learning, structured reasoning, and formal reliability guarantees into deployable decision-support systems.
